Natural on keys or touch.

Vault
Press the up arrow or swipe up to clear barriers and broken sections.
Slide
Press the down arrow or swipe down to pass beneath low hazards.
Pause
Use P, Esc, or the on-screen pause control. Hidden tabs pause an active run automatically.
